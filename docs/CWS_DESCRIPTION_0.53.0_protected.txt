✨ LISA's CORE PRINCIPLE: TRANSLATION, NOT TRUNCATION

The compression pipeline is a translator. It translates a human conversation into a signal written for machines. And like any translation, the question is: what carries meaning, and what is just grammar holding the sentence together?

Noise falls into three categories:

Structural glue: "the", "and", "with", "also", "just". These words connect ideas for human readers but carry little meaning on their own. An AI reading "tokens lean lisa conversation fix session" understands essentially what "the tokens in the lean lisa conversation to fix the session" says. The glue is for humans; the AI rebuilds it from context.

Quantifiers without anchors: "all", "two", "some", "many". They modify something, but alone they point to nothing. "Two edits" carries signal; "two" alone doesn't.

Narrative scaffolding: "verified", "noticed", "showed", "because". These describe the act of discovering something, not the thing itself. "We only noticed after the second test that it broke" is a story about what happened. "Always test before you publish" is a rule. The receiving AI needs rules, not stories.

Signal is the opposite: words that are definite, specific and meaningful on their own:
• Domain nouns: "tokens", "compression", "anchor", each names a thing
• Action verbs in context: "fix", "strip", "merge", each names what happened
• Named entities: "LISA", "Paris", "Amar", each names who or what

Stacked together, signal words form a semantic skeleton the AI expands back into full understanding. Removing the glue doesn't remove meaning; it removes repetition the AI regenerates from context.

This is what a translator does. You don't translate word for word from Arabic to English; you translate meaning. Some Arabic particles have no English equivalent and get dropped; some English articles have no Arabic equivalent and get added. The meaning crosses, the grammar adapts. That is what LISA does between human language and the AI's.

A fixed word list isn't enough: what counts as glue depends on the conversation. "File" is meaningful in a cooking conversation and noise in a coding session where every other sentence mentions files. LISA adapts its noise filter to each conversation.

An AI doesn't read words: it reads tokens, word fragments produced by a tokenizer. Every token costs compute, and raw conversation is full of waste: "sure", "got it", "thanks" use tokens that carry no meaning. Pronouns like "he", "it", "the tool" make the AI guess what they refer to. The same long name repeated forty times costs forty times the tokens. None of that ambiguity was yours; it comes from how conversation flows, not from what you meant.

LISA's pipeline works on each layer of that waste. It keeps the turns that carry the conversation and drops the noise. It writes long, repeated names once in full and keeps a glossary, so nothing is lost. It links pronouns to the people and things they refer to ("he said it was done" becomes "Amar said the parser was done"). And it counts real tokens, so you see what you actually save. The result: a leaner input in which more of the tokens carry meaning.

🧠 ACTIVE CONTEXT MANAGEMENT (ACM): keep long conversations sharp, and take them anywhere

Long conversations drift. The AI forgets early decisions, repeats itself and loses the thread. LISA watches your conversation and helps you keep it on track.

• Context health at a glance: a dot on the LISA button turns from green to yellow to red as a conversation grows.
• One-click Checkpoint (recommended): LISA asks the AI to sum up where you stand (decisions, open questions, constraints, next steps) right in your chat. No extra cost: the AI does the remembering.
• Handoff to a fresh session or another AI: LISA prepares your conversation (compact, or word for word if you prefer) and opens a new chat with everything attached as a file. Continue on the same platform or a different one.
• Checkpoints make handoffs smarter: every checkpoint travels in its place in the conversation, and the new session continues from your latest "next steps".
• Your platforms, your choice: in Settings, pick which AI platforms appear as handoff destinations.
