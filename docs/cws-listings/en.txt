LISA saves your AI conversations and lets you continue them anywhere.

Works on 12 AI platforms: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Mistral, DeepSeek, Copilot, Perplexity, HuggingChat, Meta AI, Poe and Claude Code.

✨ WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Save any conversation as readable Markdown, or as a LISA file with integrity hashes that show whether it was changed later.
• Compress long conversations: the beginning and the end stay word for word, the middle is condensed to what matters (decisions, open questions, code, rules).
• Hand off a conversation to a fresh session or to another AI, which picks up where you left off.
• Star the messages that matter: hover a message and click ☆. A starred message, and the answer to a starred question, stay word for word.
• Checkpoint a long chat: LISA asks the AI to sum up decisions, open questions and constraints, in your conversation's language.
• Choose what to save: the whole conversation, only your messages, only the AI's replies, or the messages you tick.
• Keep a library in your browser and inject a saved conversation into any chat.

🧩 THE LISA SUITE
One key and one credit balance for the extension, LISA Nexus (web library and AI compression) and Inference Shield (checks whether a text reveals your technology before you publish it).

🔒 PRIVACY
Your library stays in your browser. Text leaves it only when you choose an online feature (AI compression, sync to LISA Nexus, Inference Shield).
